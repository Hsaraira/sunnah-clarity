## The Short Answer

Yes. Devotional songs and poetry with lawful meanings — praise of the Prophet ﷺ, remembrance of Allah, exhortation to good — are generally permitted and can be spiritually beneficial. The Prophet ﷺ listened to poetry, encouraged it, and the Companions sang in gatherings and on journeys.

## What Makes a Nasheed Permissible

SeekersGuidance consistently evaluates nasheeds by their elements rather than their label:

- **Lyrics must be lawful** — remembrance, praise, admonition; not content that stirs desires or promotes sin
- **Voice-only or with duff** is the safest category; adding prohibited instruments brings the ruling of those instruments
- **The environment matters** — no free-mixing conducive to temptation, no accompanying vices
- **It should not distract from obligations** — prayer, family duties, seeking knowledge

## What About "Effects" and Production?

Modern nasheeds sometimes include layered vocals, echo, and rhythmic effects that make them sound close to instrument-accompanied music. SeekersGuidance recognizes these as real-world gray zones and advises *taqwa*-based discernment: if what you are listening to effectively mimics forbidden instruments, caution is warranted. If it remains voice and duff, the general permission applies.
