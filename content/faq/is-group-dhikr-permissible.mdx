## The Short Answer

{/* CONTENT PLACEHOLDER — Structural scaffolding. Actual content to be written by a qualified human author. */}

Yes. Collective dhikr is explicitly supported by multiple authentic hadiths. The Prophet ﷺ said:

> "No people gather to remember Allah but the angels surround them, mercy covers them, tranquility descends upon them, and Allah mentions them to those who are with Him." — Sahih Muslim

This is not an ambiguous text — it directly describes the virtue of gathering for the remembrance of Allah.

## The Evidence

In addition to the hadith above, Sahih al-Bukhari records that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Allah has angels who roam the roads, seeking those who remember Allah. When they find people remembering Allah, they call out to each other: 'Come to what you seek!'"

Imam al-Nawawi dedicates sections of *al-Adhkar* to collective remembrance and its merits.
