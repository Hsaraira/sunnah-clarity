## The Short Answer

The *duff* (دف) is a frame drum — a simple, flat hand drum traditionally without jingles or cymbals. It is widely treated as an exception to the general prohibition on instruments because the Prophet ﷺ explicitly permitted it. He said: "The difference between the lawful and the unlawful [in celebrations] is the voice and the duff" (Sunan al-Tirmidhi; Sunan al-Nasa'i).

## When Is It Allowed?

Scholars differ on whether the duff is limited to weddings and Eid or extends to general occasions of celebration and devotion. SeekersGuidance's conditions for its use include:

- It is the small traditional hand instrument, not a large drum
- The lyrics sung with it are spiritually uplifting and lawful
- The gathering maintains proper Islamic decorum
- If used in a mosque, the community is informed and consenting, and it is timed outside prayer and study

The Prophet ﷺ allowed two girls to sing and play the duff in his own home on Eid. When Abu Bakr (رضي الله عنه) objected, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Leave them, O Abu Bakr, for these are the days of Eid" (Sahih al-Bukhari; Sahih Muslim).
