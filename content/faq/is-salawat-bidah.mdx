## The Short Answer

Absolutely not. Sending blessings upon the Prophet ﷺ (*salawat*) is commanded by Allah in the Quran: "Indeed, Allah and His angels send blessings upon the Prophet. O you who believe, send blessings upon him and greet him with peace" (Quran 33:56). It is one of the most established and rewarded acts of worship in Islam.

## The Quranic Command

This is not a recommendation — it is a direct divine command. Allah Himself sends blessings upon the Prophet ﷺ, the angels send blessings upon him, and Allah then commands the believers to do the same. No one can reasonably call this a bid'a.

## The Prophetic Encouragement

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever sends one blessing upon me, Allah sends ten blessings upon him" (Sahih Muslim, no. 384). He also said that the closest people to him on the Day of Judgment would be those who sent the most blessings upon him.

He encouraged salawat on Fridays especially: "Increase your sending of blessings upon me on Fridays, for your blessings are presented to me" (Sunan Abu Dawud; graded sahih).

## What About Specific Formulas?

Various formulas of salawat have been composed by scholars throughout Islamic history — such as the Salat al-Ibrahimiyya (recited in every prayer), Dala'il al-Khayrat by Imam al-Jazuli, and others. These are all ways of fulfilling the Quranic command and are celebrated across the Muslim world.
