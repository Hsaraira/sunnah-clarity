## The Short Answer

{/* CONTENT PLACEHOLDER — Structural scaffolding. Actual content to be written by a qualified human author. */}

Yes. Tasawwuf (Sufism) is the traditional Islamic science of spiritual purification — the pursuit of *ihsan* (spiritual excellence). It has been an integral part of Sunni Islam since the earliest generations.

The greatest scholars of Islamic history — Imam al-Ghazali, Imam al-Nawawi, Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani, Imam al-Suyuti, and many others — all practiced and affirmed tasawwuf.

## What Tasawwuf Actually Is

Tasawwuf is not a separate religion or sect. It is the inward dimension of Islam that deals with sincerity, humility, gratitude, patience, and love of Allah. It corresponds to the third dimension of the Hadith of Jibril: *ihsan*.

The claim that Sufism is "foreign to Islam" or "borrowed from other religions" has no basis in Islamic scholarship. It contradicts the explicit practice of the greatest Sunni scholars across all four madhabs.
