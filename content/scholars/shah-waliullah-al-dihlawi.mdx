**Shah Waliullah al-Dihlawi** analyzed differences between the schools and argued for principled appreciation of *ijtihad* while respecting taqlid for laypeople. His work influenced later South Asian debates on madhhab and hadith.

He represents sophisticated pre-modern reflection on why scholars differ.
