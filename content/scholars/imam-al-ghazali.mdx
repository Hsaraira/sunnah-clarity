**Imam al-Ghazali**, titled *Hujjat al-Islam* (Proof of Islam), wrote *Ihya Ulum al-Din* (Revival of the Religious Sciences), integrating worship, ethics, and purification of the heart with Shafi'i jurisprudence. His work shaped Sunni spirituality and education for centuries.

Later masters — including Imam al-Nawawi — engaged deeply with his legacy. He represents orthodox Sunni *tasawwuf* grounded in law and theology.
