**Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani** is widely regarded as the greatest commentator on *Sahih al-Bukhari*. *Fath al-Bari* combines linguistic precision, narrator analysis, and jurisprudence. He also wrote influential works in hadith terminology and biography.

Traditional scholarship says that after *Fath al-Bari*, comprehensive commentary on Sahih al-Bukhari reached a summit few could rival. He represents mainstream Sunni hadith science at its height.
