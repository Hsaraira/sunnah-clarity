**Al-Izz ibn Abd al-Salam**, called *Sultan al-Ulama*, systematized the five-category analysis of *bid'a* (obligatory, recommended, neutral, disliked, forbidden) that Imam al-Nawawi and others applied in their commentaries.

This framework shows that *bid'a* in Sacred Law is not a single blanket ruling but depends on how an act relates to revelation.
