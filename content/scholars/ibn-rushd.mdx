**Ibn Rushd** (grandson of the famous philosopher) wrote *Bidayat al-Mujtahid*, mapping how the four schools derive rulings from shared evidence. The book is a standard reference for understanding *ikhtilaf*.

He shows disagreement as a structured outcome of methodology, not arbitrary opinion.
