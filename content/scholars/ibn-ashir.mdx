**Ibn Ashir** wrote *al-Murshid al-Mu'in*, a concise North African primer that weaves Maliki fiqh, Ash'ari theology, and spiritual adab. It is memorized and taught from Morocco to West Africa.

The text shows how ordinary curricula presented *tasawwuf* as part of basic education, not an optional add-on.
