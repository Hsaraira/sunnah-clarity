**Al-Khatib al-Baghdadi** wrote *al-Kifaya fi Ilm al-Riwaya*, a comprehensive manual on the ethics and method of transmitting knowledge. His *Tarikh Baghdad* preserves biographies of narrators.

He represents the self-conscious discipline of *isnad* as moral and technical trust.
