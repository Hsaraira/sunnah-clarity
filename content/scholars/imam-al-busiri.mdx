**Imam al-Busiri** composed the *Burda*, a celebrated ode in praise of the Prophet ﷺ, memorized and sung in Sunni communities worldwide. Such poetry is part of mawlid and mawlid-adjacent culture where means stay within lawful remembrance.

His work illustrates how love of the Prophet ﷺ found classical literary expression.
