**Imam Muslim** compiled *Sahih Muslim*, the second of the *Sahihayn* (the two most authentic collections). Together with al-Bukhari, his work forms the backbone of Sunni hadith-based law and theology.

Imam al-Nawawi's commentary on *Sahih Muslim* is among the most widely studied works in madrasas worldwide.
