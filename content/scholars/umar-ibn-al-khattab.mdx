**Umar ibn al-Khattab**, the second rightly guided caliph, unified the community in night prayer of Ramadan (*tarawih*) behind one imam, calling this gathering *ni'mat al-bid'a* — an excellent innovation — meaning a good new way in worship without contradicting revelation.

Classical scholars use this hadith to show that not every novelty is blameworthy; classification depends on evidence.
