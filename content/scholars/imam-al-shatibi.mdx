**Imam al-Shatibi** developed a powerful analysis of the purposes of law (*maqasid*) and warned against *bid'a* that undermines established worship. His rigor is cited by scholars on various sides of specific debates.

He is not a "modern outsider" but a major Maliki *usuli* voice.
