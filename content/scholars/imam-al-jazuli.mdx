**Imam al-Jazuli** compiled *Dala'il al-Khayrat*, one of the most beloved collections of salawat on the Prophet ﷺ, recited and copied across the Muslim world. It reflects the mainstream Sunni emphasis on abundant blessings upon the Prophet.

Such formulas are discussed in classical works like al-Nawawi's *al-Adhkar* and modern SeekersGuidance answers on salawat.
