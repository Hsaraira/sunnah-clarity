**Imam al-Qushayri** wrote *al-Risala*, a foundational text presenting *tasawwuf* as discipline of the heart within Sunni theology and law. It maps stages of the soul and corrects excess while affirming the Sunna.

The work is a standard reference for understanding why classical fiqh and *tasawwuf* were not opposed but intertwined.
