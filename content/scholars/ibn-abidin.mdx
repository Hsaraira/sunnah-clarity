**Ibn Abidin** authored *Hashiyat Ibn Abidin* (supercommentary on *Durr al-Mukhtar*), a central reference for late Hanafi law. His name stands for detailed application of Hanafi principles in the Ottoman-era Levant.

He is often cited alongside other late giants when discussing how all four schools integrated spirituality and law.
