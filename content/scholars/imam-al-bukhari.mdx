**Imam al-Bukhari** applied extraordinary criteria to narrator criticism and text comparison, producing *Sahih al-Bukhari*, held by Sunni scholarship as the most authoritative book after the Quran. His work anchors how hadith authenticity is understood.

Generations of jurists and hadith specialists built on his collection; *Fath al-Bari* by Ibn Hajar is the classic supercommentary on it.
