**Imam al-Zuhri** was among the pivotal figures who moved prophetic reports from scattered memory into organized written records consulted by caliphs and jurists. His warning that knowledge is religion — so watch from whom you take it — is quoted on this site's scholarly tradition page.

He belongs to the generation that made *isnad* discipline central.
