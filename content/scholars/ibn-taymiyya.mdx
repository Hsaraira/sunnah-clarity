**Ibn Taymiyya** was a major Hanbali scholar whose fatwas and treatises influenced later movements. On some issues — including aspects of the mawlid — later scholars note qualifications or concessions in his writings that opponents sometimes overlook.

Understanding him requires reading in context; mainstream Sunni institutions continue to cite him selectively alongside the four schools.
