**Ibn al-Salah** wrote the classic introduction to hadith sciences (*Muqaddima*), organizing how narrators are graded, how chains work, and how hadith relates to fiqh. Imam al-Nawawi summarized it in *al-Taqrib*.

His work is a pillar of how *isnad* and *diraya* are taught in traditional curricula.
