**Ibn Abd al-Barr** was a major Andalusian scholar of hadith and Maliki law. *Jami Bayan al-Ilm* gathers evidence on the virtue of knowledge and the obligation to refer to experts — themes central to *taqlid* discussions.

Imam al-Nawawi cites lines of argument that intersect with this scholarly culture.
