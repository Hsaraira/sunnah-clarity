**Imam al-Suyuti** was among the most prolific scholars in Islamic history, writing on Quran, hadith, fiqh, language, and history. His treatise *Husn al-Maqsid fi Amal al-Mawlid* argues for the permissibility of honoring the Prophet's birth when done with lawful means.

His works remain reference points across Sunni disciplines.
