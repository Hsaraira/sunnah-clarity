**Zakariyya al-Ansari** was chief judge in Egypt and authored widely read Shafi'i manuals. Traditional biographies list him among jurists who were also masters of spiritual path (*tasawwuf*).

He exemplifies the mainstream Sunni pattern that law and purification of the heart went together.
