**Ibn al-Qayyim** wrote influential works on the soul's journey (*Madarij al-Salikin*) and prophetic medicine and worship (*Zad al-Maad*). A leading student of Ibn Taymiyya, he is widely read in Hanbali and Salafi circles yet also discussed in broader Sunni discourse on *dhikr* and devotion.

His *al-Wabil al-Sayyib* on softening the heart connects to themes of remembrance and reflection.
