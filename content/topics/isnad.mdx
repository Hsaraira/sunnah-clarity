## What Is Isnad?

{/* CONTENT PLACEHOLDER — Structural scaffolding. Actual content to be written by a qualified human author. */}

*Isnad* (إسناد) literally means "support" or "chain." In Islamic scholarship, it refers to the chain of narrators through which a hadith or piece of knowledge has been transmitted, connecting it back to its original source — ultimately the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

The isnad system is one of the most sophisticated verification methods in the history of human knowledge transmission. Abdullah ibn al-Mubarak (d. 181 AH) said:

> "The isnad is part of the religion. Were it not for the isnad, whoever wished could say whatever he wished."

## Isnad vs. Silsila

While *isnad* typically refers to the chain of hadith transmission, *silsila* (سلسلة) refers to a spiritual chain — the lineage of teacher-student relationships through which Islamic knowledge and spiritual guidance have been passed down.
