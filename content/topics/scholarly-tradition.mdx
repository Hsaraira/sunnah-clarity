## Why Scholars Matter

{/* CONTENT PLACEHOLDER — Structural scaffolding. Actual content to be written by a qualified human author. */}

The Quran itself directs Muslims to seek knowledge from those who know:

> "Ask the people of knowledge if you do not know." — Quran 16:43

The scholarly tradition is not a luxury — it is the mechanism through which the Quran and Sunnah have been preserved, understood, and applied for fourteen centuries.

## The Great Scholars

The history of Islamic scholarship is extraordinary in its depth and continuity. Among the most influential:

- **Imam Abu Hanifa** (d. 150 AH) — systematized legal reasoning
- **Imam Malik** (d. 179 AH) — preserved the practice of Medina
- **Imam al-Shafi'i** (d. 204 AH) — codified the principles of jurisprudence
- **Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal** (d. 241 AH) — champion of hadith-based scholarship
- **Imam al-Bukhari** (d. 256 AH) — compiled the most authenticated hadith collection
- **Imam al-Ghazali** (d. 505 AH) — revived the religious sciences
- **Imam al-Nawawi** (d. 676 AH) — master commentator and hadith scholar
- **Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani** (d. 852 AH) — greatest commentator on Sahih al-Bukhari
