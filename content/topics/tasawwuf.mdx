## What Is Tasawwuf?

{/* CONTENT PLACEHOLDER — Structural scaffolding. Actual content to be written by a qualified human author. */}

*Tasawwuf* (تصوف) — often translated as "Sufism" — is the dimension of Islamic practice concerned with the purification of the heart and the attainment of *ihsan* (excellence in worship). It corresponds to the third pillar of the famous Hadith of Jibril: after islam (practice) and iman (belief) comes ihsan — "to worship Allah as though you see Him, and if you cannot see Him, then know that He sees you."

## The Three Dimensions of the Religion

The Hadith of Jibril (Sahih Muslim) establishes three dimensions:

1. **Islam** — the outward practice (prayer, fasting, pilgrimage, etc.)
2. **Iman** — the inward beliefs (God, angels, books, messengers, etc.)
3. **Ihsan** — the spiritual excellence (worshipping with awareness of Allah)

Tasawwuf is the science that deals with the third dimension. Just as fiqh systematizes the rules of practice and aqida systematizes the tenets of belief, tasawwuf systematizes the path to spiritual excellence.
